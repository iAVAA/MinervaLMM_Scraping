					<div id="mw-content-text" class="mw-body-content"><div class="mw-subjectpageheader">
</div><div class="mw-content-ltr mw-parser-output" lang="en" dir="ltr"><div class="shortdescription nomobile noexcerpt noprint searchaux" style="display:none">Shared path network in Milton Keynes, England</div>
<p class="mw-empty-elt">

</p>
<figure class="mw-default-size" typeof="mw:File/Thumb"><a href="/wiki/File:Fomer_Bradwell_Halt,_July_2009.JPG" class="mw-file-description"><img src="//upload.wikimedia.org/wikipedia/commons/thumb/d/da/Fomer_Bradwell_Halt%2C_July_2009.JPG/250px-Fomer_Bradwell_Halt%2C_July_2009.JPG" decoding="async" width="190" height="253" class="mw-file-element" srcset="//upload.wikimedia.org/wikipedia/commons/thumb/d/da/Fomer_Bradwell_Halt%2C_July_2009.JPG/500px-Fomer_Bradwell_Halt%2C_July_2009.JPG 2x" data-file-width="2736" data-file-height="3648" /></a><figcaption>A redway runs along the disused track bed of the former <a href="/wiki/Wolverton_to_Newport_Pagnell_Line" class="mw-redirect" title="Wolverton to Newport Pagnell Line">Wolverton to Newport Pagnell Line</a>.</figcaption></figure>
<p>The <b>Milton Keynes redway system</b> (locally known as <i>redways</i>) is an over 200 miles (320 kilometres) network of <a href="/wiki/Shared_use_path" class="mw-redirect" title="Shared use path">shared use paths</a> for cyclists and pedestrians in <a href="/wiki/Milton_Keynes" title="Milton Keynes">Milton Keynes</a>, Buckinghamshire, England.<sup id="cite&#95;ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>1<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup> It is generally surfaced with red <a href="/wiki/Asphalt_concrete" title="Asphalt concrete">tarmac</a>, and criss-crosses most of the city.
</p><p>Some of these redways run next to the <a href="/wiki/Milton_Keynes_grid_road_system" title="Milton Keynes grid road system">grid roads</a> and local roads, with underpasses or bridges where they intersect major roads. Others run through park land and along the floodplain of the Great Ouse and its tributaries.
</p><p>Construction of the redway commenced in the 1970s with the <a href="/wiki/History_of_Milton_Keynes#Milton_Keynes_Development_Corporation:_designing_a_city_for_250,000_people" title="History of Milton Keynes">start of the construction of the "new city"</a>. By 1980 it was the largest urban cycleway system in the UK with 22 miles (35.4&#160;km) in use.<sup id="cite&#95;ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>2<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup>
</p>
<meta property="mw:PageProp/toc" />
<div class="mw-heading mw-heading2"><h2 id="The_Redway_Code">The Redway Code</h2><span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">[</span><a href="/w/index.php?title=Milton_Keynes_redway_system&amp;action=edit&amp;section=1" title="Edit section: The Redway Code"><span>edit</span></a><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">]</span></span></div>
<figure class="mw-default-size" typeof="mw:File/Thumb"><a href="/wiki/File:Starship_Robot_in_Winter.jpg" class="mw-file-description"><img src="//upload.wikimedia.org/wikipedia/commons/thumb/0/08/Starship_Robot_in_Winter.jpg/250px-Starship_Robot_in_Winter.jpg" decoding="async" width="130" height="173" class="mw-file-element" srcset="//upload.wikimedia.org/wikipedia/commons/thumb/0/08/Starship_Robot_in_Winter.jpg/330px-Starship_Robot_in_Winter.jpg 2x" data-file-width="3024" data-file-height="4032" /></a><figcaption>A <a href="/wiki/Starship_Technologies" title="Starship Technologies">Starship Technologies</a> delivery robot, with its camera array visible</figcaption></figure>
<p>The redway system is restricted to cyclists, pedestrians, and powered wheelchairs.<sup id="cite&#95;ref-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-3"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>3<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup> It may not be used by horses (except where they run along designated bridleways), motorcycles, mopeds or motor-scooters ("e-scooters"),<sup id="cite&#95;ref-4" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-4"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>4<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup> though (walking-pace) electric delivery robots are permitted.<sup id="cite&#95;ref-5" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-5"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>5<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup><sup id="cite&#95;ref-6" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-6"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>6<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup>
</p>
<div class="mw-heading mw-heading2"><h2 id="National_Cycle_Network">National Cycle Network</h2><span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">[</span><a href="/w/index.php?title=Milton_Keynes_redway_system&amp;action=edit&amp;section=2" title="Edit section: National Cycle Network"><span>edit</span></a><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">]</span></span></div>
<figure class="mw-default-size mw-halign-left" typeof="mw:File/Thumb"><a href="/wiki/File:Milton_Keynes_Redway.gif" class="mw-file-description"><img src="//upload.wikimedia.org/wikipedia/commons/thumb/3/37/Milton_Keynes_Redway.gif/250px-Milton_Keynes_Redway.gif" decoding="async" width="250" height="217" class="mw-file-element" srcset="//upload.wikimedia.org/wikipedia/commons/thumb/3/37/Milton_Keynes_Redway.gif/500px-Milton_Keynes_Redway.gif 2x" data-file-width="625" data-file-height="543" /></a><figcaption>Cycleway network in Milton Keynes. NCR routes 6 and 51 are highlighted in red. (Extracted from Openstreetmap.org <br />© OpenStreetMap contributors).</figcaption></figure>
<p>The national <a href="/wiki/Sustrans" class="mw-redirect" title="Sustrans">Sustrans</a> <a href="/wiki/National_Cycle_Network" title="National Cycle Network">National Cycle Network</a> <a href="/wiki/National_Cycle_Route_6" title="National Cycle Route 6">Route 6</a> (Derby – Luton) and <a href="/wiki/National_Cycle_Route_51" title="National Cycle Route 51">Route 51</a> (Harwich - Cambridge – Oxford) runs to and through the city.
</p><p>Route 6 enters Milton Keynes from the south following the <a href="/wiki/Grand_Union_Canal" title="Grand Union Canal">Grand Union Canal</a> in southern <a href="/wiki/Bletchley" title="Bletchley">Bletchley</a>. After a loop through central Bletchley close to <a href="/wiki/Bletchley_railway_station" title="Bletchley railway station">Bletchley railway station</a> and <a href="/wiki/Bletchley_Park" title="Bletchley Park">Bletchley Park</a>, it resumes its track northwards via <a href="/wiki/Fenny_Stratford" title="Fenny Stratford">Fenny Stratford</a> along the valley of the <a href="/wiki/River_Ouzel" title="River Ouzel">River Ouzel</a> (near The <a href="/wiki/Open_University" title="Open University">Open University</a> campus and <a href="/wiki/Milton_Keynes_University_Hospital" title="Milton Keynes University Hospital">Milton Keynes University Hospital</a>) and the Grand Union to <a href="/wiki/Campbell_Park" title="Campbell Park">Campbell Park</a> (where it intersects <a href="/wiki/National_Cycle_Route_51" title="National Cycle Route 51">National Cycle Route 51</a> heading west to <a href="/wiki/Central_Milton_Keynes" title="Central Milton Keynes">Central Milton Keynes</a> and <a href="/wiki/Milton_Keynes_Central_railway_station" title="Milton Keynes Central railway station">Milton Keynes Central railway station</a>). At <a href="/wiki/Great_Linford" title="Great Linford">Great Linford</a>, the route heads west  along the former <a href="/wiki/Wolverton_to_Newport_Pagnell_Line" class="mw-redirect" title="Wolverton to Newport Pagnell Line">Wolverton to Newport Pagnell Line</a> (now a <a href="/wiki/Rail_trail" title="Rail trail">rail trail</a>). At <a href="/wiki/Old_Wolverton" class="mw-redirect" title="Old Wolverton">Old Wolverton</a> (near <a href="/wiki/Wolverton_railway_station" title="Wolverton railway station">Wolverton railway station</a>), the route crosses the <a href="/wiki/River_Great_Ouse" title="River Great Ouse">River Great Ouse</a> to leave the <a href="/wiki/Milton_Keynes_Urban_Area" class="mw-redirect" title="Milton Keynes Urban Area">Milton Keynes Urban Area</a> for the rural villages of <a href="/wiki/Castlethorpe" title="Castlethorpe">Castlethorpe</a> and <a href="/wiki/Hanslope" title="Hanslope">Hanslope</a> before crossing into <a href="/wiki/Northamptonshire" title="Northamptonshire">Northamptonshire</a>.
</p><p>Route 51 enters the east of the city at <a href="/wiki/Wavendon" title="Wavendon">Wavendon</a> then turns north at <a href="/wiki/Walton,_Milton_Keynes" title="Walton, Milton Keynes">Walton</a> alongside the Grand Union to Willen Lake then west again to cross NCR6 at Campbell Park. It ascends westwards again through the Park to <a href="/wiki/Central_Milton_Keynes" title="Central Milton Keynes">Central Milton Keynes</a>, leaving the central business district beside <a href="/wiki/Milton_Keynes_Central_railway_station" title="Milton Keynes Central railway station">Milton Keynes Central railway station</a>. The route runs south by the 'teardrop lakes' to the <a href="/wiki/National_Bowl" title="National Bowl">National Bowl</a> then west again past <a href="/wiki/Furzton" title="Furzton">Furzton</a> Lake, leaving the city at <a href="/wiki/Tattenhoe" title="Tattenhoe">Tattenhoe</a>.
</p><p>Cycle storage can be found along route 51 at Milton Keynes Central train station (covered cycle racks) and at the junction of Midsummer Boulevard and Witan Gate where there are storage and changing facilities available. There are also frequent Sheffield cycle racks near the station, and outside the shopping centre and theatre, on both sides of Midsummer Boulevard. Cyclists appear to be encouraged to cycle through car parks (with two-way lanes) on each side of Midsummer Boulevard, and use pedestrian underpasses at the major junctions (cars use the roundabouts and/or traffic lights).
</p><p>The <a href="/wiki/Swan%27s_Way_(footpath)" title="Swan&#39;s Way (footpath)">Swan's Way</a> long-distance footpath also uses part of the redway system.
</p>
